About China Tea House

China Tea House is a free, independent guide to Chinese tea. We help tea drinkers explore the world of Chinese tea with clear, practical information.

Our Mission

Making Chinese Tea Accessible

Chinese tea can feel overwhelming. Hundreds of teas, dozens of regions, and countless brewing traditions make it hard to know where to start. We organize Chinese tea into clear categories, explain the basics, and let you compare teas side by side so you can find what you love.

Editorial Method

How the Information Is Maintained

China Tea House is an independent editorial and data project. Tea profiles are organized from a structured reference dataset so category, origin, flavor, caffeine, brewing, and price fields can be compared consistently.

Those fields are starting points, not universal measurements. Harvest, cultivar, storage, water, leaf grade, and preparation can all change the cup. Price ranges are market orientation—not current offers—and caffeine labels are unverified descriptions rather than laboratory results. None of the current tea records contains a per-record source citation.

The site uses templates and structured data to generate reference pages. Selected guides include primary research with its scope stated beside the link; this does not verify every tea profile. We do not publish seller ratings, customer reviews, or product claims that we have not collected. When a page lacks a primary source, it says so through its editorial note. Corrections and source suggestions are welcome through the contact page.

What You'll Find

Tea Profiles, Comparisons & Guides

  • Detailed profiles for green, oolong, black, pu'er, white, yellow, dark, and scented teas.
  • Side-by-side comparisons to help you choose between teas.
  • Brewing guides for every major tea category.
  • Practical articles on caffeine, storage, gifts, tasting, and teaware.
