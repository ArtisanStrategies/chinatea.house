What the Evidence Can Tell You

Testing of commercial white, green, and black teas found overlapping caffeine levels. Tea color alone did not establish a reliable ranking. Product, leaf amount, water volume, temperature, and infusion time matter. That study does not measure the teas listed here or establish a ranking for oolong or pu'er.

Chin et al.: caffeine in commercial teas · Astill et al.: product and preparation factors · Read the practical caffeine guide

If you need a particular caffeine limit, seek product-specific test information for a stated brewing method. Do not use these labels as a dose filter.
