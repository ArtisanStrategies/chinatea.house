# Success Metrics: China Tea House

The owner selected a minimum of **seven Google Search clicks per week** on October 1, 2026. The objective is regular traffic: meet this minimum in four consecutive, non-overlapping seven-day windows, then maintain it.

Use Search Console property totals for Google Web Search, all countries and devices. Use Pacific dates and allow three days for reporting latency. A rolling seven-day view is an early diagnostic; assess sustained performance with weekly windows ending on the same weekday. Do not sum query, device, country, and page breakdowns into site totals. Missing stored data is unknown, not zero.

The weekly SEO report shows the latest seven-day window, the preceding seven days, the seven-click minimum, and stored data coverage. Refresh authorized Search Console data before making decisions. Account-level evidence and audit records remain private.

Prioritize pages already earning relevant impressions, improve the answer and its supporting evidence, and strengthen useful internal paths. Inspect indexing and matched page/query/country/device evidence before choosing a change. Recheck after deployment and recrawl. Keep a change log and compare complete periods; distinguish observation from a causal claim.

Secondary diagnostics are relevant impressions and positions, submitted index coverage, broken routes and references, and evidence coverage. No visitor analytics provider is currently configured in this repository.

A click target does not justify publishing unreviewed pages, invented measurements, ratings, or sources. Follow the [publication policy](docs/seo-publication-policy.md). The old 100-click script remains a legacy milestone check.
